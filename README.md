Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 20 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

One 1024×1024 newspaper front page summing up the world news of Friday,
20 November 2026 in six stories:

1. US, China & Russia hold their first-ever trilateral summit in Shenzhen
   and issue a "peace declaration"; Europe rejects a "new Yalta".
2. Russia's massive reprisal on Ukraine — 400+ drones and 110+ missiles
   after the Crimea Bridge attack; 8 killed in Kyiv.
3. A tanker ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz as Iran steps up attacks on
   Gulf shipping.
4. The ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya;
   Myanmar rejects the verdict.
5. Cyclone Akvan makes landfall on India's Odisha coast; 370,000 people
   evacuated.
6. AI stocks slide after Anthropic's record $2 trillion debut
   (S&P 500 −2%, Nikkei −4.8%).

## Run

    node main.ts

Writes `entry.png` (the front page, 1024×1024) to `/output`, or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set. The program uses only the Node.js
standard library and produces byte-identical output on every run.

## How it was made

The six panel illustrations were generated as text-free editorial
artwork; the masthead, headlines and captions were then composited
programmatically so every word stays crisp and legible.
